Human Body

+1. Head & Neck
Facial & Cranial Muscles: Frontalis, orbicularis oculi, orbicularis oris, and masseter.

Neck: Sternocleidomastoid, trapezius (upper portion), and thyroid cartilage/larynx area.

+2. Upper Body & Torso
Chest (Pectoral Region): Pectoralis major and pectoralis minor.

Abdomen: Rectus abdominis (the "abs"), linea alba (center midline), and external obliques.

Ribcage Side: Serratus anterior.

Shoulders: Deltoid (anterior, lateral, and posterior heads).

+3. Arms & Hands
Upper Arm: Biceps brachii, brachialis, and triceps brachii.

Forearm: Brachioradialis, flexor carpi radialis, and pronator teres.

Right Hand: Raised with extended index finger (pointing gesture).

Left Hand: Relaxed at the side showing extensor tendons.

+4. Lower Body & Legs
Hips & Pelvis: Tensor fasciae latae, pectineus, and iliopsoas.

Thighs (Quadriceps & Adductors): Rectus femoris, vastus lateralis, vastus medialis, sartorius, and gracilis.

Lower Legs: Tibialis anterior, gastrocnemius (calf), soleus, and extensor digitorum longus.

Feet: Metatarsals, phalanges, and extensor tendons resting on the base.


Heart
+Heart Body (Myocardium)
 Comprising the lower ventricles (left and right) and upper atria.

+Apex
 The pointed bottom end of the heart.

+Aortic Arch / Major Vessels
 The large tubular structures extending from the top represent the main blood vessels attached to the heart 

Respiratory system + Digestive system

+Trachea (windpipe) – the tube with cartilage rings.
+Right lung
+Left lung
+Main bronchi – branches of the trachea entering the lungs.
+Heart – located between the lungs, partially hidden.
+Diaphragm – muscle separating the chest and abdominal cavities.
Digestive system
+Liver – large organ below the right lung.
+Gallbladder – small sac underneath the liver.
+Stomach – curved organ beneath the liver, on the left side of the body.
+Spleen – small organ beside the stomach.
+Pancreas – elongated gland behind/below the stomach.
+Small intestine – many coiled loops in the center.
+Large intestine (colon) – thicker tube surrounding the small intestine.
+Appendix – small narrow projection near the beginning of the large intestine.
+Rectum – lower end of the large intestine.
+Anus – terminal opening at the bottom.
